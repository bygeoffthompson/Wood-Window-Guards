About

Wood Window Guards are handcrafted in Maryland.

The product came from living life with our own cats. We wanted to leave windows open without worry and nothing on the market was simple enough. Most arrive unassembled, many require adhesives or penetrations, and none of them look pleasant. So we built our own wooden window guards and eventually started building them for others.
